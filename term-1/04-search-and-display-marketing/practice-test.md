## Part A · Multiple-choice questions

1. The company that ran the first well-known pay-per-click keyword auction in 1998 was:
   a) Google b) GoTo.com (later Overture) c) Microsoft d) DoubleClick
2. CPM pricing means the advertiser pays for every:
   a) click b) 1,000 impressions c) sale d) video view
3. In programmatic advertising, the platform that helps **publishers** sell their ad space is the:
   a) DSP b) SSP c) DMP d) Search Console
4. In a second-price auction with bids of PKR 500, 450 and 300 CPM, the winner pays about:
   a) PKR 300 b) PKR 451 c) PKR 500 d) PKR 1,250
5. Which is a privacy-first response to the decline of third-party cookies?
   a) Buying data lists b) Contextual targeting and consented first-party data c) Hiding the cookie banner d) Fingerprinting users
6. Which pair correctly contrasts SEO and PPC?
   a) SEO is instant; PPC is slow b) SEO traffic continues after you stop spending for a while; PPC traffic stops when the budget stops c) Both are free d) PPC appears only below organic results
7. Advertiser A bids PKR 100 with Quality Score 8; advertiser B bids PKR 150 with Quality Score 5. Using Ad Rank ≈ bid × QS:
   a) B ranks higher b) A ranks higher c) They tie d) Neither can show
8. The **headline** limit in a responsive search ad is:
   a) 15 characters b) 30 characters c) 90 characters d) 25 words
9. Which search can a **phrase match** keyword "tax consultant" trigger?
   a) "tax consultant in lahore" b) "income consultant" c) "tax return form pdf" d) "accountant jobs"
10. Which is **not** a Quality Score component?
    a) Expected CTR b) Ad relevance c) Landing page experience d) Daily budget
11. A furniture shop with a product feed in Merchant Center that wants online sales should usually start with:
    a) Video only b) Shopping or Performance Max c) App campaign d) Display only
12. A YouTube bumper ad is:
    a) up to 6 seconds and non-skippable b) 30 seconds and skippable c) a text ad d) an image banner
13. With a 20% gross margin, break-even ROAS is:
    a) 2 b) 4 c) 5 d) 20
14. A display campaign has CTR 3% from mobile game apps but almost no conversions. The first action is to:
    a) raise bids b) check the placements report and exclude low-quality apps c) remove the conversion tag d) switch to CPM
15. Which strategy helps reduce the impact of ad blockers?
    a) More pop-ups b) Native and less intrusive formats c) Auto-playing sound d) Larger banners
16. In GA4, everything a user does is recorded as:
    a) a hit b) an event c) a goal d) a filter
17. A GA4 session lasting 7 seconds with 2 page views and no key event is:
    a) engaged b) not engaged c) a bounce d) invalid
18. Which UTM parameter should contain values like `cpc`, `email` or `paid_social`?
    a) utm_source b) utm_medium c) utm_campaign d) utm_term
19. Which tool shows the **real** queries, impressions and clicks for your own site in Google Search?
    a) Semrush b) SpyFu c) Google Search Console d) WordStream benchmarks
20. SpyFu is best known for:
    a) competitors' paid keywords and ad-copy history b) email automation c) GA4 tagging d) logo design

## Part B · Practical / scenario questions

**Scenario 1 – The tuition academy's first Google Ads campaign.** A Karachi O/A-Level academy spent **PKR 120,000** last month on one search campaign with one ad group containing 60 broad-match keywords and no negatives. Results: **48,000 impressions, 1,440 clicks, 18 enquiries**. The search terms report shows many clicks from "o level past papers pdf", "teacher jobs karachi" and "free online classes". Each enrolment is worth **PKR 60,000** in fees with a **40% margin**, and about **1 in 3** enquiries enrol.

(a) Calculate CTR, CPC, conversion rate and cost per enquiry. (b) Calculate the cost per enrolment and say whether the campaign is profitable. (c) Give a restructured plan (ad groups, keywords, negatives, landing pages, bidding).

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) CTR = 1,440 ÷ 48,000 × 100 = **3%** · CPC = 120,000 ÷ 1,440 = **PKR 83.33** · CR = 18 ÷ 1,440 × 100 = **1.25%** · Cost per enquiry = 120,000 ÷ 18 = **PKR 6,667**.
(b) Enrolments = 18 ÷ 3 = 6 → cost per enrolment = 120,000 ÷ 6 = **PKR 20,000**. Profit per enrolment before ads = 60,000 × 0.40 = PKR 24,000 → it is profitable, but only by PKR 4,000 per student (total profit after ads = 6 × 24,000 − 120,000 = **PKR 24,000**). Cutting waste would improve it a lot.
(c) **Ad groups:** "O Level Maths", "O Level Physics/Chemistry", "A Level Business", "O Level Crash Course". **Keywords:** phrase/exact such as "o level maths tuition karachi", [o level tuition near me]. **Negatives:** pdf, past papers, jobs, vacancy, salary, free, notes, solved. **Assets:** call, sitelinks (Fees, Timetable, Results, Free Demo), callouts ("Small Batches", "Cambridge-Experienced Teachers"). **Landing pages:** one per subject with fees, timetable, results, reviews, WhatsApp button and a short form. **Tracking:** form + WhatsApp + call as key events. **Bidding:** Maximise conversions once ~30 conversions/month, then target CPA around PKR 5,000.

</details>

**Scenario 2 – Is Display worth it?** An online skincare store's monthly report (gross margin 50%):

| Campaign | Cost (PKR) | Impressions | Clicks | Purchases | Revenue (PKR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search | 200,000 | 90,000 | 5,400 | 162 | 648,000 |
| Shopping | 150,000 | 300,000 | 6,000 | 150 | 600,000 |
| Display | 100,000 | 2,000,000 | 4,000 | 20 | 80,000 |

GA4 shows Display was an **assisting** touchpoint in 70 other purchases (worth PKR 280,000), and branded searches rose 25% in the months Display ran.

(a) Calculate CTR, CPC, CPA and ROAS for each campaign and break-even ROAS. (b) Should the store cut Display? Justify with numbers. (c) Suggest three Display improvements.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ 0.5 = **2.0**.
Search: CTR 6%, CPC PKR 37.0, CPA PKR 1,235, ROAS **3.24**.
Shopping: CTR 2%, CPC PKR 25, CPA PKR 1,000, ROAS **4.0**.
Display: CTR 0.2%, CPC PKR 25, CPA PKR 5,000, ROAS **0.8** (last-click).
(b) Don't cut it blindly. Last-click ROAS is 0.8, but Display assisted 70 purchases worth PKR 280,000, and branded searches rose 25%. Even if only half the assisted value is credited, Display's ROAS ≈ (80,000 + 140,000) ÷ 100,000 = **2.2**, just above break-even. Run a **holdout test** (pause Display in some cities for 4 weeks) to measure the real lift before deciding.
(c) Focus on **remarketing** to product viewers and cart abandoners; exclude low-quality app placements; set a frequency cap (3–5/week); use responsive display ads with strong product images and an offer; exclude recent purchasers.

</details>

**Scenario 3 – Measurement and competitor research for a new e-commerce launch.** A Lahore shoe brand is launching its website before Eid. It will promote it on Google Search, Instagram, an email list and posters with QR codes in two malls. It has PKR 300,000 for the first month and two established competitors.

(a) Write a GA4 setup checklist (at least 6 items) and four UTM-tagged links (Instagram, email, both QR posters). (b) Describe how you would use Semrush/SpyFu to plan the first Search campaign. (c) Using an expected CR of 2%, CPC of PKR 30 and target of 150 orders, calculate the Search budget needed and say how much is left for other channels.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) **Checklist:** create property (PKR, Karachi time zone/GMT+5), web data stream with enhanced measurement; install one tag via GTM; send e-commerce events `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase` with `value`/`currency`/`items`; mark `purchase` and `generate_lead` (WhatsApp) as key events; data retention 14 months; internal-traffic filter; link Google Ads and Search Console; cookie banner + Consent Mode; test in Real-time/DebugView.
**Links:**
`https://brand.pk/eid?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=eid-launch-2026&utm_content=reel-1`
`https://brand.pk/eid?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=eid-launch-2026&utm_content=hero-button`
`https://brand.pk/eid?utm_source=poster&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=eid-launch-2026&utm_content=emporium-mall`
`https://brand.pk/eid?utm_source=poster&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=eid-launch-2026&utm_content=packages-mall`
(b) Semrush Keyword Gap (Pakistan database) vs both competitors → list missing, lower-KD, transactional keywords ("khussa for women price", "eid sandals online"); Advertising Research / SpyFu to see which keywords competitors bid on and which ad messages have run longest (likely profitable: free delivery, COD, exchange policy). Avoid their most expensive head terms at launch; build ad groups around specific product types; write RSAs with distinct offers; add their brand-name searches only if policy-compliant and without misusing trademarks in ad text.
(c) Clicks = 150 ÷ 0.02 = **7,500**; Search budget = 7,500 × 30 = **PKR 225,000** (≈ PKR 7,500/day). Remaining = 300,000 − 225,000 = **PKR 75,000** for Instagram ads and poster printing; email is low-cost. Review after two weeks and shift budget towards the channel with the lowest CPA in GA4.

</details>
