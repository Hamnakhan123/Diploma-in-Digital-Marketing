## Part B · Practical / scenario questions

**Scenario 1 – Break-even and campaign justification.** A skincare brand sells a serum for PKR 3,200. Variable cost is PKR 1,400 per bottle, fixed monthly costs are PKR 720,000. The team wants a PKR 180,000 influencer campaign. (a) Find the current break-even quantity. (b) Find the new break-even including the campaign. (c) How many extra bottles must the campaign sell to pay for itself?

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

Contribution = 3,200 − 1,400 = PKR 1,800.
(a) 720,000 ÷ 1,800 = **400 bottles**.
(b) (720,000 + 180,000) ÷ 1,800 = **500 bottles**.
(c) 500 − 400 = **100 extra bottles** (= 180,000 ÷ 1,800).

</details>

**Scenario 2 – STP and positioning.** You are launching an online Quran-and-Arabic tutoring service. Propose three segments, choose one target with a reason, and write a positioning statement using the template *For [target], [brand] is the [category] that [difference] because [proof].*

<details><summary>✅ Model answer</summary>

Segments: (1) overseas Pakistani families in the UK/Gulf wanting children taught in their time zone; (2) local working professionals learning Arabic; (3) parents in Pakistan of children aged 6–12.
Target: segment 1 – higher ability to pay, strong need for flexible timing, reachable through Facebook community groups.
Positioning: "For overseas Pakistani parents, **NoorLearn** is the online tutoring service that schedules qualified female and male teachers in your local evening hours because our teachers work rotating shifts from Karachi."

</details>

**Scenario 3 – Funnel diagnosis and PESO plan.** A furniture store's month: 80,000 ad impressions → 1,600 site visits → 96 showroom-visit bookings → 24 purchases. (a) Compute each stage conversion and identify the weakest step. (b) Propose one paid, owned, earned and shared action to fix it.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) Visits/impressions = 2.0% · bookings/visits = 6.0% · purchases/bookings = 25.0%. The weakest step relative to benchmarks is **visit → booking (6%)**: people arrive but don't book.
(b) Paid: retarget site visitors with a "Book a showroom tour, get a free cushion" ad. Owned: add a one-click WhatsApp booking button and real room photos with prices on product pages. Earned: invite home-décor bloggers to review the showroom. Shared: run a "#MyLivingRoom" photo contest so real customer rooms appear on product pages.

</details>
