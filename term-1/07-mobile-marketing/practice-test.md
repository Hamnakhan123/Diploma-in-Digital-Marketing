## Part A · Multiple-choice questions

1. According to DataReportal (late 2025), Pakistan had about how many cellular mobile connections?
   a) 54 million b) 117 million c) 194 million d) 256 million
2. In late 2025, Pakistan's median mobile download speed compared with fixed broadband was:
   a) slower b) faster c) the same d) unknown
3. "Pharmacy near me open now" is which micro-moment?
   a) I want to know b) I want to go c) I want to do d) I want to buy
4. A Progressive Web App (PWA) is:
   a) a native iOS app b) a website that can behave like an app c) an SMS gateway d) a QR generator
5. A dynamic QR code is preferred for a billboard because:
   a) it is smaller b) the destination can change and scans can be tracked c) it needs no phone d) it never expires
6. Which is NOT a key element of a mobile marketing strategy?
   a) Audience and context b) Tracking and attribution c) Permission and compliance d) The colour of the office walls
7. A campaign spends PKR 240,000 for 4,000 installs. CPI is:
   a) PKR 6 b) PKR 60 c) PKR 600 d) PKR 16.7
8. Of those 4,000 installs, 1,000 place a first order. Cost per first order is:
   a) PKR 60 b) PKR 240 c) PKR 2,400 d) PKR 24
9. A healthy LTV : CAC ratio is commonly said to be at least:
   a) 1 : 3 b) 1 : 1 c) 3 : 1 d) 30 : 1
10. Deep links:
    a) send users to a specific screen inside an app b) shorten URLs c) block spam d) compress images
11. How many characters fit in one GSM-7 SMS?
    a) 70 b) 140 c) 153 d) 160
12. Adding one emoji to a 120-character English SMS makes it:
    a) still 1 GSM-7 segment b) Unicode, needing 2 segments c) free d) undeliverable
13. Which body regulates telecom marketing messages in Pakistan?
    a) SECP b) PTA c) SBP d) PEMRA
14. Bluetooth beacon marketing requires:
    a) nothing from the user b) the brand's app, Bluetooth on and permissions c) an email list d) a landline
15. Pay-per-call is best suited to:
    a) impulse snack purchases b) services where customers prefer to talk, like AC repair or tutoring c) mobile games d) newsletters
16. Showing an ad to people near a competitor's store is called:
    a) geotargeting b) geofencing c) geoconquesting d) beaconing
17. The best way to ask for push-notification permission is:
    a) the system prompt at first launch b) a pre-permission screen explaining the benefit, then the system prompt c) by SMS d) never ask
18. A churn model with AUC 0.86 is:
    a) random b) good at separating churners from stayers c) useless d) perfect
19. A churn model flags 400 users; 240 really churn; 600 users churned in total. Recall is:
    a) 60% b) 40% c) 24% d) 66%
20. Which is responsible AI practice in mobile marketing?
    a) Letting AI raise push frequency without limits b) Collecting contacts "just in case" c) Hard frequency caps, consent and a holdout group d) Publishing AI copy without review

<details><summary>✅ Answers</summary>

1-c · 2-b · 3-b · 4-b · 5-b · 6-d · 7-b · 8-b · 9-c · 10-a · 11-d · 12-b · 13-b · 14-b · 15-b · 16-c · 17-b · 18-b · 19-b · 20-c

**Workings:** Q7: 240,000 ÷ 4,000 = **PKR 60**. Q8: 240,000 ÷ 1,000 = **PKR 240**. Q12: Unicode allows 70 per single SMS; 121 characters → ⌈121 ÷ 67⌉ = **2 segments**. Q19: 240 ÷ 600 = **40%** (precision would be 240 ÷ 400 = 60%).

</details>

## Part B · Scenarios

**Scenario 1 – Mobile strategy for a regional bank's wallet app.** A bank wants 50,000 new wallet users in southern Punjab in 3 months. Many target customers use low-end Android phones, prefer Urdu, and some have no internet. Budget: PKR 4 million.

(a) Write the audience/context and value proposition. (b) Recommend a channel mix (at least five channels) with reasons, including at least one for offline users. (c) If the blended CPI is PKR 70 and 40% of installs complete account opening, how many installs and accounts will the budget buy? Does it meet the goal? (d) List four KPIs beyond installs.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) Adults 20–50 in Multan, Bahawalpur, Rahim Yar Khan; low-end Android, prepaid data, Urdu/Saraiki; many unbanked, value safety and simplicity. Value proposition: "Open a free account in 5 minutes with your CNIC – send money, pay bills and top up from your phone, in Urdu."
(b) Google App campaigns and Meta/TikTok install ads with Urdu video (scale); influencer/creator explainers in Saraiki and Urdu (trust); agent and branch QR standees ("scan to install") (offline-to-mobile); opted-in SMS in Urdu to existing bank customers with a download link (reaches basic/low-data users); IVR/voice and agent assistance for offline customers; push and in-app onboarding nudges for installs that haven't completed account opening.
(c) Installs = 4,000,000 ÷ 70 ≈ **57,143**; accounts = 57,143 × 40% ≈ **22,857** – the goal of 50,000 *users* is met only if "users" means installs. If the goal means opened accounts, the plan needs a higher completion rate (simpler KYC, agent help) or more budget (~PKR 8.75 M at these rates).
(d) Account-opening completion rate, cost per opened account, first transaction within 7 days, Day-30 active users, average transactions per user, and cost per funded account.

</details>

**Scenario 2 – SMS and QR launch for a restaurant.** A Hyderabad restaurant has 12,000 opted-in SMS subscribers and will put QR posters in three locations for its new delivery menu. It wants to send this SMS: "نیا ڈیلیوری مینو! 20% رعایت صرف اس ہفتے 🍛 آرڈر: wa.me/92xxxxxxxxxx" at PKR 0.60 per segment.

(a) Estimate segments and cost, check it against the opt-out requirement, and suggest a cheaper compliant version. (b) Explain how to make the QR posters trackable and effective. (c) With 95% delivery, 5% click-through, 25% ordering and AOV PKR 1,400 at 35% margin, estimate SMS profit once the required opt-out text is added (2 segments).

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) Urdu script and an emoji force **Unicode**. The message is about 67 characters, so it *just* fits in **1 segment** (limit 70): 12,000 × 1 × 0.60 = **PKR 7,200**. But adding a single word (e.g. "STOP لکھیں" for opt-out, which you must include) pushes it past 70 characters to **2 segments** (PKR 14,400). Cheaper compliant options: shorten the Urdu text so the opt-out still fits in 70 characters, or use Roman Urdu in GSM-7: "Naya delivery menu! 20% off sirf is hafte. Order: wa.me/92xxxxxxxxxx STOP to opt out" (84 characters, 1 segment).
(b) One dynamic QR or UTM-tagged URL per location (`utm_source=poster&utm_medium=qr&utm_content=location`), clear CTA ("Scan for 20% off delivery menu"), mobile menu page with WhatsApp button, high contrast and correct size for viewing distance, test on several phones, review scans per location after two weeks and move weak posters.
(c) Orders = 12,000 × 0.95 × 0.05 × 0.25 ≈ **142.5**; gross profit = 142.5 × 1,400 × 0.35 ≈ **PKR 69,825**; minus the 2-segment cost of PKR 14,400 → ≈ **PKR 55,425** profit. A compliant 1-segment version would earn ≈ PKR 62,625 – a 13% improvement from editing alone.

</details>

**Scenario 3 – AI-driven re-engagement for a ride-hailing app.** Monthly active riders have dropped 12%. The marketing team wants to send everyone a 30% discount push every day for two weeks.

(a) Explain the risks of this plan. (b) Design an AI-assisted alternative using churn prediction, value segments, send-time optimisation and guardrails. (c) How would you measure success fairly?

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) Discounts to loyal riders who would ride anyway waste money; daily pushes cause fatigue, opt-outs and uninstalls; it trains users to wait for discounts; no way to know what really worked.
(b) Build a churn model (days since last ride, ride frequency, cancellations, complaints, push settings). Segment by risk × value: high-risk/high-value → personal offer (e.g. 20% off next 3 rides) and a short survey; high-risk/low-value → reminder of new features or safety improvements, no discount; low-risk → normal communication, no extra messages. Use send-time optimisation (commute hours), max 2 marketing pushes per week, quiet hours 23:00–08:00, stop when a ride is taken, and human approval of all copy.
(c) Keep a random **holdout group** in each segment that gets nothing extra; compare rides per user, reactivation rate, revenue net of discount, opt-outs and uninstalls between treated and holdout groups over 4–6 weeks. Success = incremental rides and profit, not opens.

</details>
