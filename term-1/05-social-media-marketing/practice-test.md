## Part A · Multiple-choice questions

1. According to DataReportal's *Digital 2026: Pakistan* report, which platform had the largest advertising reach in Pakistan in late 2025?
   a) Facebook b) TikTok (adults 18+) c) Instagram d) YouTube
2. Among Facebook, Instagram and YouTube in Pakistan, which had the **highest female share** of its adult ad audience?
   a) Facebook b) Instagram c) YouTube d) All equal
3. Share of voice is:
   a) followers ÷ posts b) your brand's mentions ÷ all mentions in the category × 100 c) likes ÷ reach d) reach ÷ impressions
4. A post reached 40,000 accounts and received 1,600 likes, 120 comments, 180 shares and 300 saves. Its engagement rate by reach is:
   a) 4.0% b) 5.5% c) 7.5% d) 2.2%
5. Which bio follows the recommended formula best?
   a) "Official page. Follow us." b) "Fresh cakes in Multan 🎂 · Custom orders in 24 h · Delivery across the city · WhatsApp to order 👇" c) "We are the best" d) "Bakery"
6. In the HEARD method for complaints, the step that fixes the root cause internally is:
   a) Hear b) Apologise c) Resolve d) Diagnose
7. During a brand crisis, scheduled promotional posts should be:
   a) boosted b) paused c) duplicated d) left running
8. In Meta Ads Manager, where are audience, budget (without campaign budget), placements and optimisation event set?
   a) Campaign b) Ad set c) Ad d) Business profile
9. A 1% lookalike audience of past purchasers is:
   a) people who already bought b) new people most similar to past purchasers c) website visitors d) a random audience
10. A campaign shows 600,000 impressions to 240,000 people. Frequency is:
    a) 0.4 b) 2.5 c) 4.0 d) 360,000
11. With a 45% gross margin, break-even ROAS is about:
    a) 1.45 b) 2.22 c) 4.5 d) 0.45
12. Compared with lowest-cost bidding, a strict bid cap usually gives:
    a) higher CPA and more spend b) lower CPA but possibly much less spend delivered c) identical results d) free clicks
13. Social **listening** (not just monitoring) would be:
    a) replying to a DM about prices b) noticing delivery complaints doubled over three months and changing courier c) hiding a spam comment d) liking a customer's photo
14. A brand has 400 mentions: 180 positive, 140 neutral, 80 negative. Net sentiment is:
    a) +25 b) +45 c) +20 d) −25
15. PAS stands for:
    a) Problem, Agitate, Solve b) Post, Analyse, Share c) Plan, Act, Scale d) Price, Audience, Sale
16. A YouTube video has 11% impressions CTR but only 15% average percentage viewed. The most likely problem is:
    a) bad thumbnail b) title/thumbnail promise not delivered or slow start c) too many tags d) no subscribers
17. A 10-minute video gets 12,000 views with an average view duration of 5 minutes. Its watch time is:
    a) 600 hours b) 1,000 hours c) 2,000 hours d) 60,000 hours
18. Social media ROI should be calculated with:
    a) revenue and ad spend only b) gross profit and all costs (ads, content, staff, tools, creators) c) likes and followers d) reach and impressions
19. In an A/B test, A gets 300 clicks from 30,000 impressions and B gets 390 from 30,000. z ≈ 3.45. At 95% confidence:
    a) not significant b) B is significantly better c) A is significantly better d) cannot tell without revenue
20. Which is responsible use of AI in social media?
    a) Generating fake customer reviews b) Pasting customer phone numbers into a public chatbot c) Using AI to draft 30 hooks, then fact-checking, editing and A/B testing the best d) Letting a chatbot argue with angry customers

<details><summary>✅ Answers</summary>

1-b · 2-c · 3-b · 4-b · 5-b · 6-d · 7-b · 8-b · 9-b · 10-b · 11-b · 12-b · 13-b · 14-a · 15-a · 16-b · 17-b · 18-b · 19-b · 20-c

**Workings:** Q4: (1,600 + 120 + 180 + 300) ÷ 40,000 × 100 = 2,200 ÷ 40,000 = **5.5%**. Q10: 600,000 ÷ 240,000 = **2.5**. Q11: 1 ÷ 0.45 = **2.22**. Q14: (180 − 80) ÷ 400 × 100 = **+25**. Q17: 12,000 × 5 ÷ 60 = **1,000 hours**. Q19: |z| = 3.45 > 1.96 → significant; B's CTR 1.30% vs A's 1.00%.

## Part B · Scenarios

**Scenario 1 – Launching a modest-fashion brand on social media.** A Lahore brand selling abayas and modest wear (AOV PKR 6,000, gross margin 50%) wants to launch nationally. Budget: PKR 400,000 for the first month, including content. The founder wants to be "on every platform".

(a) Using the platform reach and gender data, recommend 2 core platforms and 1 support channel, with reasons. (b) Write the Instagram bio and list 5 Story Highlights. (c) Define 4 content pillars with percentages and give two post ideas for each. (d) The founder wants 60 orders from ads. With CR 1.5%, CTR 1.2% and CPM PKR 220, calculate the ad budget needed and check it against break-even CPA.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) **Instagram** (34.9% female share – the highest after YouTube among big feeds; visual, Reels, shopping, creators) and **TikTok** (largest reach, ≈23 M women by estimate; try-on and styling videos). **WhatsApp Business** as support (orders, catalogue, COD). Facebook can come later for older women and Groups; YouTube for long styling guides once content capacity grows. Being "on every platform" spreads a small team too thin.
(b) *"Modest wear made in Lahore 🧕 · Breathable fabrics for Pakistani weather · COD nationwide · Shop the new edit 👇"*. Highlights: *New in · Sizes · Prices · Reviews · How to order*.
(c) Educate 35% (fabric guides; "how to style one abaya 3 ways"), Entertain 25% (behind the scenes of stitching; outfit transition trend), Community/UGC 25% (customer try-ons; Q&A on sizing), Promote 15% (launch countdown; Eid pre-order offer).
(d) Clicks = 60 ÷ 0.015 = **4,000**; impressions = 4,000 ÷ 0.012 ≈ **333,333**; budget = 333,333 ÷ 1,000 × 220 ≈ **PKR 73,333**. CPA ≈ PKR 1,222 vs break-even CPA = 6,000 × 0.5 = **PKR 3,000** → comfortably profitable. That leaves ≈ PKR 326,000 for content production, creators and testing – sensible for a launch.

</details>

**Scenario 2 – A delivery brand with a sentiment problem.** A Karachi food-delivery brand has the largest share of voice in its category, but a listening tool shows net sentiment of −10. Themes: taste positive; delivery late/cold and "no reply to refund" negative. A viral post this morning shows a rider being rude to a customer.

(a) Explain the difference between its share of voice and its sentiment. (b) Write a crisis response plan for today (steps with timings) and the public holding statement. (c) Recommend three operational fixes and three content ideas for the next month. (d) How will you measure whether the situation improved?

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) SOV measures how much people talk about the brand (volume); sentiment measures how they feel. Large SOV with negative sentiment means lots of *bad* talk – fame for the wrong reasons.
(b) **Within 1 hour:** pause all scheduled promotional posts and ads with cheerful messages; gather facts (order, rider, customer); agree a holding statement. **Holding statement (public reply and Story):** "We've seen the video and we're sorry – this is not acceptable. We've contacted the customer and suspended the rider while we investigate. We'll share an update by 6 pm today." **By 6 pm:** update with actions taken. **Next days:** reply to every related comment with the same message, DM affected customers, monitor mentions hourly.
(c) Operations: rider conduct training and a complaint escalation number; insulated bags and delivery-time promises; WhatsApp auto-reply plus a 2-hour refund response SLA. Content: "Meet our riders" series showing training; "hot in 30 minutes" proof videos with timestamps; a transparent "how refunds work" carousel.
(d) Track net sentiment weekly (target from −10 to positive within 6–8 weeks), negative mentions about delivery and service, average response time, refund resolution time, ratings, and repeat order rate.

</details>

**Scenario 3 – Proving social ROI and testing creatives.** An Islamabad online electronics store spent in one month: ads PKR 300,000, content PKR 80,000, staff PKR 120,000, tools PKR 20,000. GA4 attributes PKR 2,400,000 revenue to social (gross margin 22%). Meta Ads Manager reports PKR 3,100,000. The owner says, "Our ROAS is over 10, social is amazing." The team also ran an A/B test: video A got 420 clicks from 35,000 impressions; video B got 455 clicks from 35,000.

(a) Calculate ROAS (GA4 basis) and true ROI. Is the owner right? (b) Explain why Meta and GA4 numbers differ and which you would use for what. (c) Is video B significantly better? Show the z calculation. (d) Suggest four ways to improve ROI next month.

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

(a) ROAS = 2,400,000 ÷ 300,000 = **8.0** (GA4; 10.3 on Meta's numbers). Total cost = 300,000 + 80,000 + 120,000 + 20,000 = **PKR 520,000**. Gross profit = 2,400,000 × 0.22 = **PKR 528,000**. ROI = (528,000 − 520,000) ÷ 520,000 × 100 ≈ **+1.5%** – barely breaking even. The owner is looking at revenue and ad spend only; with a low electronics margin, the true picture is much thinner.
(b) Meta counts view-through and multi-day click windows and credits itself; GA4 uses its own attribution (data-driven by default) across all channels and only sees tagged website sessions. Use **Meta** to optimise campaigns within Meta; use **GA4** (plus real orders) to compare channels and judge ROI.
(c) CTRs: A = 1.20%, B = 1.30%. Pooled p̂ = 875 ÷ 70,000 = 0.0125. SE = √[0.0125 × 0.9875 × (2 ÷ 35,000)] = √(7.05 × 10⁻⁷) ≈ 0.00084. z = 0.0010 ÷ 0.00084 ≈ **1.19** < 1.96 → **not significant**; keep testing or test a bigger difference.
(d) Raise AOV with accessories bundles; cut CPA by excluding past buyers and refreshing creatives; increase conversion with faster WhatsApp replies and clearer warranty/COD messaging; reduce production cost by repurposing one shoot into many posts and using UGC; and add retargeting to cart abandoners with push/WhatsApp (opted-in) reminders.

</details>
