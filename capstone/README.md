# 🎓 Capstone Projects

> 🏠 [Diploma in Digital Marketing](../README.md) › **Capstone Projects**

Each term ends with a capstone project that pulls every module together into one portfolio piece. Work individually or in teams of up to three (agree this with your lecturer). Use a real local business, a non-profit, or a realistic fictional brand.

---

## Term 1 Capstone · Integrated Digital Marketing Launch Plan

### 📌 Brief

You are the digital marketing team for a small business launching (or relaunching) online. Produce a complete, costed **90-day digital marketing plan** plus working assets.

### 📦 Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Draws on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Situation analysis: market research, competitors, SWOT, 2 buyer personas, customer journey map | Module 1 |
| 2 | Positioning statement, USP and 4Ps/7Ps | Module 1 |
| 3 | A 3-page responsive landing site (HTML5 + CSS3 + a little JavaScript, or WordPress) with a lead form and `Organization`/`LocalBusiness` schema markup | Modules 2, 3 |
| 4 | SEO plan: 20-keyword map, on-page fixes, local SEO checklist | Module 3 |
| 5 | Search & display plan: campaign structure, 3 ad groups, 6 ad variations, budget and bids | Module 4 |
| 6 | Social plan: platform choice, 4-week content calendar, 3 ad creatives, targeting | Module 5 |
| 7 | Email plan: welcome sequence (3 emails), segmentation, KPI targets | Module 6 |
| 8 | Mobile plan: one SMS/WhatsApp flow and a QR-code activation | Module 7 |
| 9 | Measurement plan: KPI tree, tracking (UTMs, GA4 events), forecast of CTR/CPC/CPA/ROAS, dashboard mock-up | Module 8 |
| 10 | AI usage plan: where AI tools help and the human checks you apply | Module 9 |
| 11 | 10-minute pitch deck and presentation | Module 1 |

### 🗓️ Milestones

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 | Business chosen, research and personas approved |
| 2 | Positioning + website wireframe |
| 3 | Website built; SEO and search plan drafted |
| 4 | Social, email and mobile plans |
| 5 | Measurement plan + budget + forecasts |
| 6 | Final report, website link and live pitch |

### 🧮 Rubric (100 marks)

| Criterion | Marks | Excellent looks like… |
|---|---|---|
| Research & insight | 15 | Data-backed personas and a clear customer problem |
| Strategy & positioning | 15 | Sharp USP; every channel ties back to it |
| Website & technical work | 20 | Valid HTML/CSS, responsive, fast, schema validates |
| Channel plans | 20 | Realistic targeting, calendars, creatives and budgets |
| Measurement & numbers | 15 | Correct formulas; sensible forecasts; clear KPIs |
| Presentation | 10 | Takeaway headlines, confident delivery, handles Q&A |
| Ethics & privacy | 5 | Consent-based data collection, honest claims |

## Term 2 Capstone · Data-Driven, AI-Assisted Growth Programme

### 📌 Brief

Take an existing (real or realistic) online business and design a **six-month growth programme** that uses advanced SEO, paid media, automation, analytics and AI, while staying fully privacy-compliant.

### 📦 Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Draws on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Technical + content SEO audit with a prioritised fix list and topic-cluster plan | Module 1 |
| 2 | Multi-platform paid media plan with campaign structure, budget allocation model and creative tests | Module 2 |
| 3 | MarTech stack diagram (CMS, analytics, CRM, automation, consent tool) with justification | Module 3 |
| 4 | Analytics notebook: CLV, cohort retention, attribution comparison and a 3-month forecast (Python or spreadsheet) | Module 4 |
| 5 | Affiliate/partner programme design: commission model, tracking, partner recruitment | Module 5 |
| 6 | Automation blueprint: lifecycle journeys, lead scoring, segmentation rules | Module 6 |
| 7 | AI strategy: use-cases, data needed, ML model choice, success metrics, dashboard | Module 7 |
| 8 | Privacy and ethics assessment: data map, lawful basis, consent flows, bias checks | Module 8 |
| 9 | Executive report (max 15 pages) and 12-minute board-style presentation | All |

### 🗓️ Milestones

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 | Business, data sources and goals agreed |
| 2 | SEO audit and MarTech diagram |
| 3 | Paid media and affiliate plans |
| 4 | Analytics notebook and AI strategy |
| 5 | Automation blueprint and privacy assessment |
| 6 | Final report and presentation |

### 🧮 Rubric (100 marks)

| Criterion | Marks |
|---|---|
| Diagnosis & audit quality | 15 |
| Strategy coherence across channels | 15 |
| Analytical rigour (correct metrics, models, forecasts) | 20 |
| Automation & AI design | 15 |
| Budget logic and ROI case | 15 |
| Privacy, ethics & compliance | 10 |
| Communication (report + presentation) | 10 |
